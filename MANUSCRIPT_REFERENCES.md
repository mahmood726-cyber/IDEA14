## REFERENCE FORMATTING NOTES

### **Vancouver Style (Circulation, most cardiology journals):**
- Numbered consecutively in order of citation
- List up to 6 authors, then "et al."
- Journal abbreviations per PubMed/Index Medicus
- Format: Author(s). Title. Journal. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages.

### **AMA Style (JAMA Cardiology):**
- Similar to Vancouver but different punctuation
- Format: Author(s). Title. Journal. Year;Volume(Issue):Pages. doi:xxx

### **Verify Before Submission:**
- ✅ All references cited in text are in list
- ✅ All references in list are cited in text
- ✅ Numbered sequentially
- ✅ Journal abbreviations correct
- ✅ Page numbers complete
- ✅ No formatting errors

### **Common Errors to Avoid:**
- ❌ Missing issue numbers
- ❌ Inconsistent journal abbreviations
- ❌ Wrong punctuation
- ❌ References cited but not listed
- ❌ References listed but not cited

### **Tools for Verification:**
- EndNote/Zotero can auto-format to journal style
- PubMed has "Cite" function with Vancouver format
- Journal websites usually have reference formatting guides
