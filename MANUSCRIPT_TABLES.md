## Table 2. Summary of Meta-Analytic Results: ICD Effectiveness Under Modern GDMT

| Statistical Method | Key Parameter | Estimate (95% CI/UI) | Interpretation | Clinical Impact |
|-------------------|---------------|----------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Component Network Meta-Analysis** | Combined effect of modern GDMT on baseline mortality | RR 0.575 (42.5% reduction) | Modern pharmacotherapy (ARNi + SGLT2i + increased MRA use) reduces baseline mortality by 42.5% compared to therapy in foundational trials (2000-2001) | Baseline annual mortality reduced from 7-12% (historical) to 3-6% (modern) |
| **Meta-Regression with Baseline Risk** | Association between baseline risk and ICD effect (β₁) | -0.394 (R² = 0.851) | For each doubling of baseline risk, ICD log(HR) decreases by 0.394 (stronger effect in higher-risk patients). Model explains 85.1% of between-trial variance. | At 5% baseline risk (modern): ICD RRR ~5%<br>At 20% baseline risk (historical): ICD RRR ~45% |
| **Predictive Interval for Future Trial** | Predicted ICD effect in new 2025 trial | HR 0.805<br>(95% PI: 0.644-1.015) | In a hypothetical new trial with contemporary GDMT, predicted 20% mortality reduction, but 95% predictive interval crosses 1.0 (no benefit) | Substantial uncertainty about benefit in modern populations; upper bound of PI indicates possibility of no mortality reduction |
| **Bayesian Posterior Predictive Distribution** | Pooled ICD effect (posterior mean) | HR 0.798<br>(95% CrI: 0.709-0.898) | Bayesian synthesis incorporating prior knowledge yields similar point estimate to frequentist methods but with full uncertainty characterization | Posterior probability of ANY benefit (HR<1.0): 80%<br>Posterior probability of substantial benefit (HR<0.8): 64% |
| **Bayesian NNT Estimate (5-year)** | Number needed to treat under modern GDMT | 30.3<br>(95% UI: -53.6 to 103.0) | To prevent one death over 5 years, ~30 patients require ICD therapy under 2025 conditions (vs. 15 in SCD-HeFT). Wide uncertainty interval reflects extrapolation beyond trial populations. | **NNT approximately doubles** compared to foundational trials<br>Increase: +102% |

**Abbreviations:** CI = confidence interval; CrI = credible interval; GDMT = guideline-directed medical therapy; HR = hazard ratio; NNT = number needed to treat; PI = predictive interval; RR = relative risk; RRR = relative risk reduction; UI = uncertainty interval.

**Footnotes:**
- Predictive intervals (PI) differ from confidence intervals: PIs estimate the plausible range in a *single future study*, whereas CIs estimate uncertainty about the *average* effect across included studies.
- Bayesian uncertainty intervals (UI) incorporate all sources of uncertainty including between-study heterogeneity and parameter uncertainty.
- Negative lower bound of NNT uncertainty interval indicates the possibility of harm (HR > 1.0), though this is unlikely based on point estimate.

**Key Finding:** All four advanced meta-analytic methods converge on the conclusion that ICD effectiveness is substantially diminished under contemporary GDMT, with NNT approximately doubling and substantial uncertainty about benefit in modern low-risk populations.
